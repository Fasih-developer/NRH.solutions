PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy and data security are our highest priorities. Read how we protect your information.

1. Data Collection

NRH Solutions gathers essential contact details provided voluntarily by our clients. This typically includes your full name, email address, telephone number, and relevant corporate details. We acquire this data strictly to facilitate seamless communication and deliver our premier marketing services.

2. Utilization of Data

The details we collect are deployed exclusively for operational and administrative functions. This encompasses responding to inquiries, providing campaign updates, confirming schedules, and delivering comprehensive customer support. We utilize your data solely for legitimate business operations.

3. SMS Communications & Compliance

By supplying your cellular number, you grant explicit consent to receive text messages from NRH Solutions concerning your active projects, service updates, and inquiries.

  • Message frequency aligns with your level of engagement with our agency.
  • Standard messaging and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier.

You maintain the right to revoke this consent at any time by replying:

  • STOP – to immediately cancel all text message subscriptions.
  • HELP – to receive assistance regarding our messaging system.

4. Strict Data Confidentiality

We categorically refuse to sell, lease, rent, or distribute your personal details—including phone numbers—to external marketing agencies or unauthorized third parties. Your information remains strictly within our internal infrastructure to facilitate our direct communication with you.

5. Authorized Service Partners

Occasionally, we may utilize secure third-party platforms (such as secure SMS gateways) solely to transmit necessary operational messages. These vendors are legally bound to uphold the strictest confidentiality and data protection standards and cannot use your data for their own purposes.

6. Information Security

We deploy robust digital safeguards and administrative protocols to shield your private information against unauthorized breaches, alterations, misuse, or unwarranted disclosures.

7. Acknowledgment of Consent

By engaging with our web forms, portals, and communication channels, you formally authorize NRH Solutions to contact you via email, telephone, or SMS strictly regarding the services you have requested.

8. Revoking SMS Consent

You retain full control over text-based communications. To halt SMS alerts immediately, simply reply with the word STOP to any message. Upon receipt, you will be permanently removed from our mobile messaging roster.

9. Policy Modifications

NRH Solutions reserves the right to amend this document to reflect shifting legal requirements or corporate protocols. All revisions will be published directly on this webpage, ensuring you are always informed.

10. Mobile Information & Opt-Out Clause

No mobile information will be shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional endeavors. All other categories inherently exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this specific information will never be sold, shared, or acquired by any third-party entity.

If you wish to be removed from receiving future communications, you can opt out at any time by texting STOP or contacting us directly at nrhsolutionsllcusa@gmail.com.
